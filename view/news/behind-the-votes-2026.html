Behind the Votes: How the Scoreboard Is Changing

Our Admin managing the servers

The Cat Contest scoreboard uses the classic two-layer system: a viewer layer where every voter spreads 20 points across all entries, and a jury layer where each national jury hands out the traditional 1-8, 10 & 12. The gap between those two layers is where the real drama lives — and this year, that gap is massive.

The juries are currently rewarding vocal precision. Tommy (Schnurr für Deutschland) leads the jury column, with Salvador Schnurral and Alexander Katzbak close behind. The viewer column tells a completely different story: Purreen, Gato Fuego and Ailuros Olympia are pulling the crowd, while the jury-favorite ballads sit several rows lower.

A few numbers stand out. Måneschnurr is the only cat placing top-three in both columns, which makes Zitti e Miau the safest overall bet. Sir Whiskers has the widest spread — beloved by three specific juries, largely ignored by the public. And Tommy is the classic "rising mid-table" case: small viewer gains every day, currently just outside the top five. One strong live performance away from breaking into contention.
