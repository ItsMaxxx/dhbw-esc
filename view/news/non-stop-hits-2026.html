The Hits are back... Non-Stop!

Two Cats fighting for their favorite Singers

The official Cat Contest 2026 playlist is back, and every single entry from this year's lineup is in there — from Tommy's stadium-ready Schnurr für Deutschland to Édith Miauf's heart-wrenching Je ne regrette purr. It is built for fans who want the contest feeling on repeat, no matter what they are doing.

And yes, the cover image with two cats going at it says it all: these hits really are back, and they are definitely not keeping it calm.The mix moves between moods without losing momentum. Gato Fuego brings the heat with Fiesta Felina, Duncan Pawrence slows it down with Arcade (Red Dot Chaser), and Ailuros Olympia cranks the tempo back up with Opa, Katze!. Between the anthems, quieter moments like Salvador Schnurral's Amar pelos dois give your ears — and probably your cat — a chance to recover.

Hit play and keep the contest spirit running all week. Fair warning: Johnny Miaugan's Hold Me Meow has a habit of getting stuck in everyone's head for days.
