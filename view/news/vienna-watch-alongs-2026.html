Join us for the Vienna 1967 & 2015 Watch-Alongs

Eurovision trophy on stage in Vienna

We're bringing two unforgettable Eurovision years back to the screen with special community watch-alongs for Vienna 1967 and Vienna 2015. Whether you love classic orchestral entries or the modern arena era, this double event is made for fans who want to relive the songs together.

During both sessions, visitors can react live, rank every performance and vote for their own winners. We'll also highlight memorable interval moments, staging details and the songs that still define Eurovision conversations today.

Expect a relaxed but highly opinionated evening full of nostalgia, surprises and score debates. Bring your favorites, defend your hot takes and join the chat as Vienna takes center stage once again.
