Join us for the Vienna 2026 Watch-Alongs

Eurovision Cat trying to watch the Semi Finals through the window

Vienna hosts the Cat Contest for the third time, and we're marking the occasion with the community watch-alongs: 2026-edition. Our German entry Tommy with Schnurr für Deutschland will kick off the first evenings with a short acoustic intro before we roll the archive footage.

During the sessions, viewers can react live, rank every performance and hand out mock points in the classic 20-point-pattern we also use in the real voting tab. Expect plenty of hot takes on whether Måneschnurr's Zitti e Miau would have held up against the 1967 orchestra, and how Tommy's Schnurr für Deutschland compares to the Stockholm staging era.

Bring your favorite cat, your sharpest scoreboard opinions, and a cup of tea worthy of Sir Whiskers. Vienna is once again the center of the Cat Contest universe — and this time, the couch counts as a front-row seat.
