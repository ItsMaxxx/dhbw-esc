Community Picks: The Songs Fans Can't Stop Voting For

Fans stacking on top of each other to keep watching the Semi Finals

Every viewer on the site assigns the classic ESC scores (1–8, 10 & 12) across the competing cats, and the first wave of votes is in. Purreen (Scratch-Tattoo) is currently collecting the highest average allocation per ballot, followed closely by Måneschnurr and Gato Fuego. No single cat dominates — most users stack their top scores on three or four favorites.

The biggest mover this week is Édith Miauf (Je ne regrette purr). Fan edits of the rehearsal chorus pushed her into the top five over the weekend, and she's now neck-and-neck with Johnny Miaugan's Hold Me Meow in the community standings. Meanwhile Tommy (Schnurr für Deutschland) is holding a steady mid-table spot — loved by the jury mock-votes, still warming up with the public.

Voting patterns also reveal regional loyalty. Viewers tend to spread their points across neighboring countries, but Sir Whiskers's Tea and Tuna and Kätzchen Mozart's Meow bei Nacht are both pulling strong cross-border support. If the trend holds into the final round, the scoreboard could be one of the tightest we've seen.
