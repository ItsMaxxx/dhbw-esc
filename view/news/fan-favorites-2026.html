Early fan favorites and bold predictions

Fan at the Voting-Pool in Vienna, days before the Grand Final

With the Grand Final just around the corner, the Cat Contest fan community has made its voice heard — and the early consensus is surprisingly clear. Måneschnurr from Sweden leads almost every fan poll by a comfortable margin, riding the wave of a dreamy indie-pop performance that left dress rehearsal audiences visibly emotional. The fan forums are full of slow-motion replays of that key change in the final chorus, and the clip has been looping on social media for days.

Right behind is Gato Fuego of Spain, whose high-energy staging — complete with live percussion and a pyrotechnic finale — has consistently drawn the longest queues at the fan village’s meet & greet tent. Italy’s Micio Stellare rounds out the top three in most aggregated rankings, though fans are divided: some call it a certain top-five, others worry the staging is too understated for the Grand Final stage.

As for bold predictions: expect Tommy (Germany) to dramatically outperform the bookmakers. Home-crowd energy in a semifinal can be worth several placing spots, and the Viennese audience has been singing along to every rehearsal. Dark-horse status also goes to Sir Whiskers (United Kingdom), whose quirky folk-ballad has divided critics but built a quietly devoted fanbase that tends to punch above its weight on voting night.

One thing the community agrees on: this year’s leaderboard could look very different after the jury votes are revealed. The fan favorites and the juries have rarely aligned at recent contests — and that gap is exactly what makes Cat Contest voting night worth staying up for.
