Early fan favorites and bold predictions

Crowd at a Eurovision-style event with colorful lights

It never takes long for Eurovision fans to build a top ten, defend dark horses and predict dramatic scoreboard twists. This year is no different, with online discussions already split between polished frontrunners and entries that could peak at exactly the right moment.

Some songs are winning early because they feel immediate, while others are growing through rehearsals, live clips and fan edits. That mix is what makes prediction season so exciting: consensus is rare, and momentum can change fast.

We're tracking the entries that fans cannot stop talking about, plus the outsiders that might turn into major stories on show night. Expect strong opinions, changing rankings and at least one result nobody saw coming.
