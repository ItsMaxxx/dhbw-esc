What to do in Vienna during Eurovision week

A Cat in Vienna's city center with the Austrian flag before show week

Vienna is the perfect Cat Contest city for fans who want more than just the live shows. Austria's own Kätzchen Mozart is playing on every café radio this week, and Meow bei Nacht has already become the unofficial anthem of the Prater district. Start your day at the fan village near the Rathaus — meet & greets with Tommy and Édith Miauf are scheduled for the first two afternoons, and rumor has it Måneschnurr will drop in for a surprise acoustic set. If you spot a cat outside happily chomping on grass, don’t worry — it’s just doing its pre-show salad course.

Cat-friendly cafés around the 1st district are running themed menus (yes, including a "Tea and Tuna" special in honor of Sir Whiskers). In the evenings, shift into the watch-party rhythm. Most bars will be streaming the semifinals, and the official fan zone hosts a short pre-show set with Gato Fuego before the live broadcast begins. Balance the headline events with time to wander — the best Cat Contest memories usually come from the detours between them, or from the cat that decided the lawn was the real green room.
