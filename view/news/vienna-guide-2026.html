What to do in Vienna during Eurovision week

Person holding an Austrian flag in a city street

Vienna is the perfect Eurovision city for fans who want more than just the live shows. Between elegant streets, music history and countless places to meet other visitors, the city gives every contest week a special atmosphere.

Start with daytime exploring, then shift into the evening rhythm of watch parties, themed bars and spontaneous fan gatherings. Even a simple walk through the center feels different when flags, playlists and ESC conversations are everywhere.

If you're planning your week, balance the big headline events with time to wander, eat well and follow the buzz. The best Eurovision trips usually come from a mix of scheduling and happy detours.
